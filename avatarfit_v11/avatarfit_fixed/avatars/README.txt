HOW TO ADD YOUR AVATAR IMAGES (for M2)
=======================================

Drop your PNG files in this folder with these exact names:

  cyber.png   — the Cyber athlete avatar
  robot.png   — the Robot avatar  
  ghost.png   — the Ghost avatar

IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
- PNG with transparent background
- Ideally square (e.g. 400x400px or 600x600px)
- The figure should face forward, arms slightly out, legs slightly apart
- The app will scale it automatically to fit the canvas

HOW IT WORKS:
- The app draws your PNG image centred on the canvas
- It overlays a glowing skeleton on top to show joint positions
- Red limbs appear on top when form is bad
- You don't need to do anything in the code — just drop the files here

If you don't have images ready, the app falls back to the 
built-in cartoon avatar automatically.
